Tabbed Lua editor with rename, per-tab persistence, keyboard shortcuts and syntax highlighting.

Script Search

Search ScriptBlox directly in the app — browse trending scripts and run them in one click.

Hub & Favorites

Built-in popular scripts plus favorites and execution history to re-run anything fast.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Download & run

Grab the bootstrapper and run it — it installs (or updates) ENIxecutor automatically.

Get your key

Open the app, press Get Key, complete the quick steps and copy the daily key.

Attach & execute

Join a game, press Inject, paste or pick a script and hit Execute.

Ready to run?

Download the bootstrapper — it installs ENIxecutor and keeps it up to date.
